# EXP-1-PROMPT-ENGINEERING-

## Aim: 
Comprehensive Report on the Fundamentals of Generative AI and Large Language Models (LLMs)
Experiment: Develop a comprehensive report for the following exercises:

Explain the foundational concepts of Generative AI.
Focusing on Generative AI architectures. (like transformers).
Generative AI applications.
Generative AI impact of scaling in LLMs.

## Algorithm:

1. Define the objective of generating a detailed report on Generative AI and LLMs.

2. Design structured prompts related to architectures, applications, and scaling laws.

3. Input prompts into Generative AI tools such as ChatGPT,Claude,or Gemini
.
4. Collect generated responses systematically.

5. Analyze the generated content for correctness, clarity, and completeness.

6. Organize the content into sections such as Introduction, Architecture, Applications, and Scaling Impact.

7. Enhance the report using tables, diagrams, graphs, and visual representations.

8. Finalize and format the report for academic submission.

## Prompt:
Generate a comprehensive, exam-ready report on the fundamentals of Generative AI and Large Language Models (LLMs). Include the following topics in detail:

1. Introduction to Generative AI

2. Transformer Architecture and its components

3. Applications of Generative AI in various industries

4. Impact of scaling laws in LLMs

5. Comparative analysis of small and large language models


6. Advantages, challenges, and future scope of Generative AI

The report should be well-structured with headings, tables, diagrams, graphs, and conclusions suitable for academic submission
